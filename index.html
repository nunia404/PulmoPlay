No mouse needed — each plot is a key: ASDFJKL;, same as the clarinet. Hold a key to plant/tend its plot (hold several to tend several at once), then hold I (release with N) to inhale — it rains on the garden and every held plot grows, just like inhaling mutes each key's clarinet note until you exhale with X. A longer, fuller breath grows more. Press a ripe plot's key again to harvest it and beat your best score!
